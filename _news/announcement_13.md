Dr. Shalaila S. Haas gave an invited talk, "AI and Psychotherapy: Insights from the PREDiCTOR Study," at the PsychE / PsychAI Symposium 2026 on April 18, 2026.

The hybrid symposium was held at Mount Sinai's Goldwurm Auditorium and brought together clinicians, researchers, and psychoanalytic scholars for a day of discussion on artificial intelligence, consciousness, psychotherapy, psychoanalysis, and society.

Shalaila's presentation focused on how AI can be used to read emotions, support clinically relevant assessment, and inform diagnosis and treatment in mental health care, drawing on insights from the PREDiCTOR Study.
