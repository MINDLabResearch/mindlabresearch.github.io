It was great to see our wonderful CRCs working on the PREDiCTOR Study present our recent work on "Optimizing Audiovisual Recording Equipment for Ai-Driven Psychiatric Research - A Feasibility Pilot Study" at the Society of Biological Psychiatry in Toronto!

**Poster** 

Title: “Optimizing Audiovisual Recording Equipment for Ai-Driven Psychiatric Research - A Feasibility Pilot Study.”

Details of the poster are available [here](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/392125738_Optimizing_Audiovisual_Recording_Equipment_for_Ai-Driven_Psychiatric_Research_A_Feasibility_Pilot_Study). 

This work could not be possible without the hard work and dedication of our amazing PREDiCTOR CRCs: Theo, Bailey, Maya, and Debi! 

**Conference Theme** 

The theme of this year's conference was “Artificial Intelligence and Mental Health: A new landscape for research and practice” and the aim was to explore the intersection of artificial intelligence (AI) and mental health. 
There were many excellent presentations throughout the conference covering many areas of how AI can be used to better understand and improve mental health!

Details about SOBP can be found [here](https://sobp.org/meetings/past-meetings/).
